serviceQueue.acquire();

// Writer requests turnstile & resource
serviceQueue.acquire();
resource.acquire();
serviceQueue.release();
sharedData++;
resource.release();
💡 Starvation Prevention: Without serviceQueue, an endless stream of readers would continually increment readCount, starving writers indefinitely. By routing both readers and writers through serviceQueue, incoming readers cannot jump ahead of an earlier queued writer!
