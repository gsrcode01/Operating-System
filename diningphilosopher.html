Dining Philosophers

Four philosophers, four chopsticks, one monitor. The monitor checks and reserves both chopsticks of a philosopher in a single step, keeps a FIFO queue of hungry philosophers, and wakes each one through a private semaphore. Click a philosopher to make them hungry, or let the simulation run on its own.
